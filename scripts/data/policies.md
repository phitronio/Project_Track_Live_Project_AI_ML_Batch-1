# Kinbo Store Policies

## About Kinbo

Kinbo is an online gadget store based in Bangladesh. We sell phones, laptops, tablets and accessories, and every price on our website is shown in BDT. We do not run physical shops for walk-in customers. Everything happens online, and our delivery partners bring the order to the customer's door.

Our promise is simple: official products, clear prices and honest answers.

Customers can reach our support team through chat and by email at support@kinbo.example. Support hours are in the CUSTOMER SUPPORT section below.

## What Kinbo Sells

Kinbo sells four kinds of products.

- Phones, in a range of budgets from simple everyday models to premium flagships.
- Laptops for study, office work, creative work and gaming.
- Tablets for reading, drawing, video and light work.
- Accessories such as chargers, cables, cases, screen protectors, earbuds, headphones, power banks, mice, keyboards and bags.

The product page on the website is the best place to see the current list, the specifications and the price. Do not invent specifications for a model you do not know. Suggest checking the product page.

## Authenticity and Official Products

All products sold by Kinbo are official. Every product comes with the brand's official warranty. We do not sell grey-market, copied or counterfeit items.

Each product is sold as a sealed, brand-packed unit. The box, seal, serial number and invoice together help prove that the product is genuine. We advise customers to keep the box and the invoice, at least for the return period and ideally for the whole warranty period, because the brand's service centre may ask for them.

If a customer believes that something they received is not genuine, they should contact customer support straight away, with photos of the product, the box and the invoice. The assistant should not decide whether a product is genuine from a chat description.

## Prices, VAT and Invoices

All prices on the Kinbo website are in BDT and include VAT. There is no separate VAT line added at checkout. The only extra charge a customer may see at checkout is the delivery charge, and that is waived for orders of 20,000 BDT or more.

The price at the moment the order is placed is the price paid. If the price of a product changes after the order is placed, the customer's order is not affected.

Every order comes with an invoice. The invoice shows the product, the price paid, the VAT-inclusive total, the delivery charge if any, and the order details. Customers should keep it safe. It is needed for change-of-mind returns and it is useful for warranty claims. If a customer needs a corrected name or business details on an invoice, they should contact customer support soon after placing the order.

## Promotions, Discounts and Price Matching

Kinbo does not offer discounts, coupons or price matching through chat. The sales assistant must never offer, hint at, negotiate or confirm any discount, coupon code, special price or price match, even if the customer says that another store is cheaper.

Any promotion that Kinbo runs is shown only on the website. If a customer asks about offers, tell them to check the website for current promotions, and remind them that the price they see when the order is placed is the price they pay.

If a customer is unhappy with a price, you may suggest a different product that fits a smaller budget, or mention EMI if the order meets the EMI rules described below.

## How Ordering Works

Ordering from Kinbo is straightforward.

- The customer browses the website, opens a product page and adds the product to the cart.
- At checkout they enter the delivery name, phone number and full address, and choose a payment method.
- They review the total, which shows the VAT-inclusive price and the delivery charge if any, and then confirm.
- The order is then placed, and the customer receives an order confirmation.

Customers should double check the phone number and address before confirming, because the courier uses these to reach them. A wrong phone number is one of the most common reasons for delivery delays.

Orders are placed only on the website. The assistant can explain each step and help the customer decide on a product.

## Payment Methods

Kinbo accepts these payment methods.

- Cash on delivery, for orders up to 50,000 BDT.
- bKash.
- Nagad.
- Debit cards and credit cards.

Customers choose their payment method at checkout. Online payments go through secure payment pages run by the payment providers. The assistant must never ask a customer to type card numbers, PINs, one-time passwords or wallet details into the chat. If a customer starts sharing such details, tell them gently not to, and explain that payments are completed only on the website.

If a payment fails or money is deducted without an order confirmation, the customer should contact customer support with the time of the payment and the amount. The assistant cannot check payment records.

## Cash on Delivery

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 50,000 BDT. For orders above 50,000 BDT, the customer should choose bKash, Nagad or a debit or credit card.

With cash on delivery, the customer pays the delivery person when the order arrives, so they should keep the right amount ready, including the delivery charge if one applies. If the outer box looks badly damaged, they should take photos and report it as described in the section on DAMAGED OR MISSING ITEMS ON DELIVERY.

Cash on delivery cannot be combined with EMI. If a cash-on-delivery order is later refunded, the refund goes to bKash or Nagad, as explained in the REFUNDS section.

## EMI and Instalments

Kinbo makes larger purchases easier through EMI. The rules are strict, so please explain them carefully and exactly.

- EMI is available only on credit cards from partner banks.
- EMI is available only for orders of 10,000 BDT or more.
- The tenures are 3 months, 6 months or 12 months.
- For 3 months and 6 months, the interest is 0%.
- For 12 months, the bank's own interest applies.
- EMI is arranged by the bank, not by Kinbo.
- EMI is not available with cash on delivery, bKash or Nagad.

Because the bank arranges the EMI, the bank decides the approval, the monthly instalment, the exact interest for the 12-month option and any card-related terms. Kinbo does not set these and the assistant cannot calculate or promise them. If a customer asks what their monthly payment will be for a 12-month plan, explain that the bank's own interest applies and that the bank can give the exact figure.

If a customer asks about EMI on a debit card, bKash, Nagad or cash on delivery, the answer is no. If the order is below 10,000 BDT, the answer is also no.

## Delivery Areas, Times and Charges

Kinbo delivers across Bangladesh. The delivery time and charge depend on where the order is going.

- Inside Dhaka: 2 working days, and the delivery charge is 60 BDT.
- Outside Dhaka: 4 working days, and the delivery charge is 120 BDT.
- Free delivery on orders of 20,000 BDT or more, both inside and outside Dhaka.

Working days are days when Kinbo and its delivery partners are operating. Public holidays do not count as working days. The delivery time starts after the order is placed.

Delivery days are estimates. They are not guarantees. Kinbo never promises an exact delivery date, and the assistant must never promise one either. Weather, traffic, courier workload, incorrect addresses or an unreachable phone number can all cause delays. Customers should keep their phone on and reachable, because the delivery person usually calls before arriving.

## Order Status and Tracking

Every order moves through three statuses.

- Placed. The order has been received and is being prepared.
- Shipped. The order has been handed to the delivery partner and is on its way.
- Delivered. The order has reached the customer.

Customers can follow the status of their order on the website. The assistant cannot see orders, so it cannot tell a customer where their parcel is. When asked, explain the three statuses, remind the customer that delivery days are estimates, and tell them to check the order status on the website.

If an order has not arrived after the estimated number of working days, the customer should contact customer support with their order number. The assistant should not blame the courier or promise a day.

## Cancelling an Order

A placed order can be cancelled before it ships. Cancellation before shipping is free of charge, and the stock of the cancelled items is returned to the shop so that other customers can buy them.

Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. It can only be returned after delivery, under the return rules in this policy.

To cancel a placed order, the customer should contact customer support as early as possible, because the order may ship soon. The assistant cannot cancel orders itself, and it cannot check whether an order has shipped. If the customer paid online for a cancelled order, the refund follows the timing and method rules in the REFUNDS section.

## Damaged or Missing Items on Delivery

Customers should open the parcel carefully and check the contents soon after delivery.

If an item arrives physically damaged, or if an item or part is missing from the parcel, the customer must report it within 48 hours of delivery, with photos. The photos should show the outer box, the shipping label, the damage or the missing part, and the product itself.

Report such problems to customer support through chat or at support@kinbo.example, with the order number. The team reviews the report and tells the customer the next step. The assistant must not promise a particular outcome, such as an instant replacement. Reports made after 48 hours may be harder to verify, so customers should not wait.

## Returns

Customers can return a product within 7 days of delivery, under these conditions.

- Change-of-mind returns are accepted only for unopened items, with the seal, the box and the invoice intact and present.
- Opened items can be returned only if they are defective on arrival, and the defect must be reported within 7 days of delivery.

So if a customer simply changes their mind after opening the box, we cannot accept the return. We encourage customers to read the specifications and ask questions before ordering.

To start a return, the customer should contact customer support within the 7 days, with the order number and a short reason. For defective items, photos or a short video of the problem help. Support will explain the next steps. The assistant cannot start returns itself and should not decide whether a return will be accepted. It can only explain the rules.

## Refunds

When a return is accepted, or when an order is cancelled after online payment, the refund is made within 5 to 7 working days. It goes to the original payment method.

- Paid by bKash: the refund goes back to bKash.
- Paid by Nagad: the refund goes back to Nagad.
- Paid by debit or credit card: the refund goes back to the same card.
- Paid by cash on delivery: the refund goes to bKash or Nagad, because cash cannot be sent back to a customer. The customer will be asked which wallet account to use.

The 5 to 7 working days are counted from when Kinbo approves the refund. If a card was used on an EMI plan, the bank manages how the instalments are adjusted, and the customer can ask their bank how the refund shows on their statement. If the refund has not arrived after 7 working days, the customer should contact customer support with their order number. The assistant cannot see payment records and should not promise a specific refund day.

## Warranty

All Kinbo products are official and come with the brand's official warranty. The warranty length is the number of months shown on each product page. It differs between products, so the assistant must not guess it. If a customer asks how long the warranty is, tell them to check the number of months shown on the product.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects only. That means problems that come from how the product was made, such as a part that fails under normal use.

The warranty does not cover any of these.

- Physical damage, such as drops, cracks, dents and bent frames.
- Liquid damage, including water, rain, tea and sweat.
- Damage caused by unofficial repair, or by opening the device outside the brand's authorised service centre.

Customers should keep the invoice, because it shows when the product was bought.

## Warranty Claims

Warranty claims go to the brand's authorised service centre. The warranty belongs to the brand, and the brand's service centre decides whether a problem is a manufacturing defect that is covered.

Kinbo helps the customer start the claim. The customer should contact customer support, share the order number and describe the problem. Support explains which authorised service centre to go to and what to bring, which normally includes the product and the invoice.

The assistant should explain this process in simple words and be careful not to promise that a claim will be accepted. A warranty claim is different from a return. A return is for the first 7 days after delivery and follows the return rules. A warranty claim is for manufacturing defects during the warranty length shown on the product.

## Trade-In and Repairs

Kinbo does not offer trade-in. We do not buy used phones, laptops or tablets, and we cannot take an old device as part payment for a new one.

Kinbo does not offer repairs. We do not repair devices ourselves. For a product bought from Kinbo, the way to get a manufacturing defect looked at is the warranty claim described above, through the brand's authorised service centre.

The assistant should say this plainly and kindly, and never suggest that a trade-in or repair is possible with a special request.

## Out of Stock and Restock Notifications

Sometimes a product is out of stock. The website shows the stock status on the product page. If a product is out of stock, the customer cannot place an order for it.

Customers who want the product can use the restock notification option on the product page of the website, if it is shown. The assistant cannot sign people up.

Never promise that an out-of-stock product will return, and never give a restock date. You may suggest similar products that are in stock.

## Accessories and Compatibility Advice

Accessories only work well when they fit the device. When customers ask for accessory advice, ask which device model they own, and then check these points.

- Cases and screen protectors are made for specific models. A case for one model will not fit another model, even if the screen size is similar.
- Chargers and cables should match the device's charging port, such as USB-C, and the charging speed it supports. A faster charger will not harm a device that supports a lower speed, but it will not charge faster than the device allows.
- Laptop bags and sleeves should match the laptop's screen size.

If you are not sure that an accessory is compatible, say so, and suggest customer support. All accessories sold by Kinbo are official and come with the brand's official warranty, with the number of months shown on the product.

## Device Setup Help

The assistant can offer general guidance for new gadgets, and the brand's own guide in the box is always the best source.

- Charge the device before first use, with the charger and cable that came with it or an official one.
- For a phone, insert the SIM card, switch on the device and follow the on-screen steps to choose a language, connect to Wi-Fi and sign in to the brand's account.
- Install system updates early, since they often include security fixes.
- Set up a screen lock, such as a PIN or fingerprint.

If a device does not switch on, suggest trying a different outlet and cable, and holding the power button for a while. If the problem remains, the customer should contact customer support quickly, so that a defect can be reported in time.

## Battery Care Tips

Good habits help a battery last longer. These are friendly tips, not guarantees.

- Avoid leaving the device in hot places, such as in direct sunlight or in a closed car. Heat is hard on batteries.
- Charging a little and often is fine. Try not to let the battery run all the way down often.
- Use an official or good quality charger and cable. Cheap, unknown chargers can harm the battery.

All batteries slowly lose capacity as they age. This is normal and is not a manufacturing defect. If a battery seems to fail unusually early, or swells, the customer should stop using the device and contact customer support about the warranty process.

## Screen and Body Care Tips

A little care keeps a gadget looking good and working well.

- Use a case and a screen protector, especially on phones and tablets. They help with everyday scratches and knocks, though they cannot prevent all damage.
- Clean screens with a soft, clean cloth, and never spray liquid directly onto the device.
- Keep devices away from water, even if they are advertised as splash resistant.
- Do not carry a laptop with a pen or charger between the screen and the keyboard, because closing the lid can crack the screen.

Physical damage and liquid damage are not covered by the warranty, so prevention is the best protection.

## Gift Orders

Many customers buy gadgets as gifts, and that is welcome. A customer can place an order for another person by entering the recipient's name, phone number and address as the delivery details.

- Cash on delivery is available for gift orders up to 50,000 BDT, and the recipient will be asked to pay on delivery.
- The return period of 7 days starts from the delivery, so the buyer and the recipient should talk early about whether the gift is right.
- Change-of-mind returns need an unopened item with the seal, the box and the invoice. Gifts should therefore not be opened until the recipient is sure they want to keep them.

## Corporate and Bulk Orders

Companies, schools, offices and groups sometimes need several devices at once. Corporate and bulk orders are welcome, and customer support can help with them.

Ask the customer to contact customer support through chat or at support@kinbo.example, with the products, the quantities and the delivery location. The team can explain the process, including the options for invoices in a company name and for delivery.

The assistant must not quote bulk prices, promise special rates or agree to any discount, because Kinbo does not offer discounts, coupons or price matching through chat. Any promotion appears only on the website. The general policies, such as official products, official warranty, payment methods, EMI rules and delivery, apply to bulk orders in the same way. Cash on delivery is limited to orders up to 50,000 BDT.

## Privacy and Data

Kinbo respects customer privacy. We collect only the information needed to process orders, such as name, phone number, delivery address and order details.

Kinbo never sells customer data. We share only what is needed with delivery partners and payment providers so that an order can be completed.

The chat assistant cannot see orders or personal data. It does not have access to a customer's account, address, phone number, payment records or order history. Customers should not share sensitive details in the chat. If someone shares a card number, PIN, password or one-time code, tell them not to, and do not repeat it.

## Customer Support

Customer support is available from Saturday to Thursday, 10 AM to 8 PM. Customers can reach support through chat and by email at support@kinbo.example. Messages sent outside support hours are read when the team is next available.

When you send a customer to support, suggest that they have these ready: the order number, the name and phone number used for the order, a short description of the problem, and photos if the issue is about damage, a missing item or a defect. Be honest about the support hours and never promise how fast the team will reply.
